## Summary

- On April 18, 2026 at 17:35 UTC (Ethereum block 24,908,285), an attacker [forged a LayerZero cross-chain message](https://www.theblock.co/post/397988/kelp-daos-rseth-bridge-apparently-exploited-for-roughly-292-million-in-layerzero-based-attack) to release 116,500 rsETH — about 15% of circulating supply, worth roughly [$290-292 million](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/kelpdao-bridge-exploit-april-2026/) — from KelpDAO's Ethereum bridge escrow in a single transaction.
- The stolen tokens were immediately pledged as collateral on Aave, Compound, and Euler; the attacker borrowed an estimated $236 million in WETH and wstETH and laundered proceeds through Tornado Cash ([Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/kelpdao-bridge-exploit-april-2026/)).
- The drain triggered mass withdrawals across lending platforms — Aave saw roughly [$6.3 billion exit](https://dailyhodl.com/2026/04/19/6280000000-abruptly-exits-ethereum-based-lending-platform-as-cross-chain-hack-triggers-mass-withdrawals/) — and DeFi total value locked fell roughly [$13 billion in two days](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/04/20/defi-tvl-drops-more-than-usd13-billion-in-two-days-following-kelp-dao-hack/) following the incident.
- Root cause was not a smart-contract bug but a compromise of off-chain LayerZero verification infrastructure: attackers poisoned the RPC backend used by the sole designated verifier ([Galaxy Research](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/kelpdao-layerzero-exploit-defi)).

## Attackers

- LayerZero preliminarily attributed the operation to the [DPRK-linked Lazarus Group, specifically its TraderTraitor subgroup](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/04/20/layerzero-blames-kelp-s-setup-for-usd290-million-exploit-attributes-it-to-north-korea-s-lazarus); this attribution was preliminary and not independently confirmed by other vendors at the time of writing.
- The drain required no human compromise: per [Galaxy Research](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/kelpdao-layerzero-exploit-defi), it used a node list, two poisoned RPC binaries, a DDoS against real endpoints, and one forged packet.

## Losses

- 116,500 rsETH (~$290-292 million, about 15% of supply) drained in a single forged release ([Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/kelpdao-bridge-exploit-april-2026/)).
- Of that, 89,567 rsETH (~$221 million) was deposited on Aave as collateral, funding loans of [82,650 WETH (~$190.9 million) and 821 wstETH (~$2.3 million)](https://governance.aave.com/t/rseth-2026/24580).
- The cashout used collateral borrowing rather than a DEX sell-off; realized value was ~$200-236 million, with roughly $175 million reaching Bitcoin rails within days per [Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/kelpdao-bridge-exploit-april-2026/).
- Aave's estimated bad debt ranged from [~$123.7 million under uniform loss socialization to ~$230 million if losses fall on bridged L2-issued rsETH](https://governance.aave.com/t/rseth-2026/24580), with ~$177 million the most-cited median.
- The bridge invariant broke: the escrow adapter fell from 116,723 to 223 rsETH while remote-chain claims stood at 152,577 rsETH, leaving approximately 152,354 rsETH unbacked — roughly a 73.5% shortfall against claims ([Galaxy Research](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/kelpdao-layerzero-exploit-defi)).

## Timeline

- **17:35 UTC, April 18, 2026:** A forged packet claiming Unichain origin was accepted by a single 1-of-1 DVN without any matching source burn, releasing 116,500 rsETH ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/397988/kelp-daos-rseth-bridge-apparently-exploited-for-roughly-292-million-in-layerzero-based-attack)).
- **April 18, 2026, within minutes:** Tokens fanned across branch addresses and into Aave plus other lending venues; early estimates topped $100 million.
- **April 18, 2026, ~46 minutes later:** A second forged packet (~40,000 rsETH, ~$95 million) reverted because the protocol had already paused contracts and frozen addresses ([Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/kelpdao-bridge-exploit-april-2026/)).
- **April 18, 2026, same day:** Aave's Protocol Guardian froze rsETH and wrapped-rsETH reserves to zero LTV across all deployments, then froze WETH borrows within a day ([Aave governance incident report](https://governance.aave.com/t/rseth-2026/24580)).
- **April 20, 2026:** The Arbitrum Security Council froze [30,766 ETH](https://x.com/arbitrum/status/2046435443680346189) of the exploiter's coins; LayerZero published a [post-mortem](https://x.com/LayerZero_Core/status/2046081551574983137).
- **By April 20, 2026:** Total DeFi TVL had fallen [$13 billion in two days](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/04/20/defi-tvl-drops-more-than-usd13-billion-in-two-days-following-kelp-dao-hack/); Aave reopened core ETH loans and loss allocation moved to governance.

## Security Failure Causes

- **Single-verifier configuration (single point of failure):** Kelp deployed rsETH with one required DVN verifier and zero optional verifiers, so a single forged attestation authorized every release with no independent check. LayerZero's post-mortem placed responsibility on this setup ([CoinDesk coverage](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/04/20/layerzero-blames-kelp-s-setup-for-usd290-million-exploit-attributes-it-to-north-korea-s-lazarus)).
- **Validation layer sat off-chain, outside audit scope:** Attackers modified the RPC backend the DVN queried, fed it fabricated burn events, and DDoS'd the genuine endpoints so the verifier only saw attacker-controlled data, per [Galaxy Research](https://www.galaxy.com/insights/research/kelpdao-layerzero-exploit-defi). Smart-contract audits did not cover this layer.
- **No cross-chain invariant monitoring:** A release on Ethereum with no corresponding burn on the source chain is invisible to per-transaction checks; detecting it requires cross-chain reconciliation comparing mints against burns, which was absent ([Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/kelpdao-bridge-exploit-april-2026/)).
- **Composability amplified the failure:** rsETH served as collateral across ~20 chains and major lending venues, so a single forged packet created bad debt at protocols never breached and drove a multi-billion-dollar withdrawal run at Aave ([Bank Policy Institute](https://bpi.com/crypto-hacks-and-defi-runs/)).
